Contributors are acknowledged according to their project responsibilities and
the work they perform.

## Contribution principles

- Credit should reflect meaningful research, engineering, documentation, data,
  testing, review, or project-management contributions.
- New contributors should be added with their preferred name and agreed role.
- Role descriptions should be updated when project responsibilities change.
- Contributors should preserve DeepGene's research-only scope and clinical
  safety boundaries.

DeepGene is a collaborative research software project. Contributions do not
turn exploratory model outputs into clinical validation or authorize clinical
use.
