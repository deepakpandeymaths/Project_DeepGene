PURPOSE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
This dataset converts the validated DeepGene V1 evidence representation into an explainable variant-level profile.
It does not assign an arbitrary pathogenicity score.
It preserves the underlying ClinVar evidence and explicitly separates evidence, reliability, phenotype, representation, and review context.

IMPORTANT METHODOLOGICAL NOTE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The evidence profile is descriptive.
It should not be interpreted as an independent pathogenicity prediction.
ClinVar clinical significance remains part of the V1 evidence representation and therefore cannot simultaneously serve as an independent benchmark label for machine-learning evaluation.
